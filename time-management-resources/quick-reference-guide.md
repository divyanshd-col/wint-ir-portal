# Time Management Quick Reference Guide

Your one-page reminder for staying on track.

---

## ⚡ The Golden Rules

1. **Energy > Time** — Protect your peak hours for your most important work
2. **3 > 10** — Three real priorities beat ten imaginary ones
3. **Plan Tonight** — Decide tomorrow's top priorities before bed (5 min)
4. **Say No Faster** — Every yes is a no to something else
5. **Batch Everything** — Group similar tasks to reduce context switching

---

## 🎯 Daily Workflow

### Night Before (5 min)
- [ ] What are my 3 priorities tomorrow?
- [ ] What time will I work on Priority #1?
- [ ] What am I NOT doing tomorrow?

### Morning (2 min)
- [ ] Review priorities
- [ ] Protect first 2-hour block for Priority #1
- [ ] Turn off notifications for focused work

### Midday
- [ ] Did I make progress on Priority #1?
- [ ] What's blocking me?
- [ ] Do I need to adjust plans?

### End of Day (5 min)
- [ ] Did I complete my top priority?
- [ ] What took longer than expected?
- [ ] Plan tomorrow's top 3

## 🧠 Peak Hours Strategy

**Find your peak:** When do you think clearest? (Usually 8am-12pm)

**Protect it fiercely:**
- No meetings during peak hours
- No email during peak hours  
- No Slack during peak hours
- Phone on Do Not Disturb
- Work on ONE thing

**Save for off-peak:**
- Email responses
- Slack/messaging
- Admin tasks
- Routine meetings
- Data entry

---

## 📦 Batching Tactics

| Task Type | Batch Frequency | Duration |
|-----------|----------------|----------|
| Email | 2-3x per day | 30 min each |
| Slack/Messages | 3-4x per day | 15 min each |
| Meetings | Cluster on 2-3 days | - |
| Calls | Back to back blocks | 2 hours |
| Admin work | Weekly block | 1-2 hours |
| Planning | Daily (evening) | 5 min |
| Deep work | Daily (peak hours) | 2-4 hours |

## ⏰ The Time-Boxing Method

Instead of working until something is "done," work for a fixed block:

**Example:**
- ❌ "I'll work on the report until it's finished" → Takes 6 hours, expands to fill time
- ✅ "I'll work on the report for 90 minutes" → Creates focus, prevents perfectionism

**How to:**
1. Set a timer for the block (30-90 min usually best)
2. Work ONLY on that task
3. When timer ends, stop and assess
4. Decide: ship it, schedule next block, or move on

## 🚫 The Art of Saying No

### Polite Scripts:

**For meetings:**
"Thanks for thinking of me. I don't think I can add much value here. Could you send me the notes after?"

**For requests:**
"I'd love to help, but I'm at capacity right now. Could we revisit this in [timeframe]?"

**For unclear requests:**
"Before I commit, help me understand: what's the goal, what's the timeline, and what exactly do you need from me?"

**The powerful pause:**
"Let me check my commitments and get back to you by [time]."
(Never say yes immediately to non-urgent requests)

## 🔥 When You're Overwhelmed

### The 10-Minute Reset:

1. **Brain dump** (2 min): Write down everything on your mind
2. **Identify the ONE thing** (1 min): What's the most important thing right now?
3. **Time-box it** (1 min): When will you work on it?
4. **Clear the decks** (1 min): What can you delete, delegate, or defer?
5. **Take action** (5 min): Start the ONE thing for just 5 minutes

### The Eisenhower Matrix:

| | Urgent | Not Urgent |
|---|---|---|
| **Important** | DO FIRST | SCHEDULE |
| **Not Important** | DELEGATE | DELETE |

Most things in "Urgent but Not Important" can be delegated or handled faster than you think.

## 📱 Technology Boundaries

### Essential Settings:
- Turn off ALL non-essential notifications
- Use Do Not Disturb during peak hours
- Set email to fetch manually (not push)
- Uninstall social media from phone (use browser only)
- Keep phone in different room during focus work

### Recommended Tools:
- Timer: Phone timer, Pomodoro app, or physical timer
- Blockers: Freedom, Cold Turkey, Screen Time
- Calendar: Block time like meetings (even for solo work)
- Notes: Simple text file for brain dumps

## 💡 Remember

- **You can't do everything** — That's not failure, that's reality
- **Margin is not waste** — Buffer time prevents cascading delays
- **Done > Perfect** — Shipping beats polishing
- **Energy compounds** — Protect sleep, exercise, and downtime
- **Systems beat motivation** — Good habits make willpower optional

---

## 🆘 Emergency Shortcuts

**When deadline is TODAY:**
1. What's the minimum viable version?
2. What can I cut without losing core value?
3. Who can help if I ask RIGHT NOW?

**When you're stuck:**
1. Set timer for 10 min and just start
2. Work on easiest part first to build momentum
3. Ask for help sooner rather than later

**When everything is urgent:**
1. Ask: "If I could only do ONE thing, what would it be?"
2. Do that thing
3. Reassess
