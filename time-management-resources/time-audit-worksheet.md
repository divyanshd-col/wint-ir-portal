# Time Audit Worksheet

## Why Do a Time Audit?
Before you can manage your time better, you need to know where it actually goes. Most people are shocked by what they discover. This worksheet helps you see the truth.

---

## 📋 Instructions

**Track for 3-5 days** (include at least one weekend day)

Set phone reminders every 2 hours to log what you've been doing. Be honest—no judgment, just data.

## 🏷️ Category Key

Use these categories to tag your activities:

- **DW** = Deep Work (focused, important tasks)
- **SW** = Shallow Work (emails, admin, meetings)
- **SN** = Social/Networking
- **PE** = Personal/Exercise
- **EN** = Entertainment/Rest
- **CM** = Commute/Travel
- **CS** = Context Switching (changing between tasks)
- **W** = Wasted (be honest!)
